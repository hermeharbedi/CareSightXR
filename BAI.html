CareSight XR

Company Narrative & Role of AI

CareSight XR is a solution designed to empower clinicians with instant, immersive access to patient data.

Mission: Empower clinicians with instant, immersive access to patient data.

Vision: A world where doctors see vitals & scans seamlessly in their field of view.

Role of AI: Patient recognition, data prioritization, intelligent visualization.

Value Proposition

  • For Doctors and Healthcare providers
  • Dissatisfied with manual scrolling of reports
  • Due to lack of platform, which can meet the needs of instant, unified access to patient vitals and scans at the point of care
  • CareSight XR offers mixed reality healthcare interface
  • That provides real-time, AI-powered patient recognition, seamless integration of vitals/reports/scans, and hands-free visualization enabling faster, safer clinical decisions

Moat & Product

  • Proprietary AI Models: We have advanced patient recognition trained specifically for hospital conditions (like masks and poor lighting), providing reliability that competitors can't easily match.
  • Deep Technical Expertise: Our solution was built by a PhD in XR with healthcare AI research experience, combining rare skills at the intersection of XR, computer vision, and clinical workflow design.
  • Seamless Integration Layer: Native support for FHIR and DICOM standards ensures smooth interoperability with existing EHR and PACS systems, reducing switching costs for hospitals.
  • Clinician-Centered Experience: The product was co-designed with a team of Veterinarian and Pediatric Doctors to ensure it fits naturally into clinical routines rather than disrupting them, for both humans and our paw friends
  • Academic–Industry Network: Strong research credibility and collaborations accelerate validation, adoption, and provide an early-mover advantage in XR healthcare.
